Propulsion Lab

Three simulated vehicles for a 90.7 kg (200 lb) rider: a wheeled skateboard with a compressed-air boost thruster, a cold-gas hoverboard and a theoretical chemical-rocket hoverboard. Every motion you see is integrated live from forces, mass flow and stored energy. Nothing is scripted.

Simulation only. High-pressure gas and rocket propulsion can kill. Nothing here is a design or build guide. Any physical test of these ideas must be professionally reviewed, unmanned and safely contained.

Skateboard: hold ACCEL for the hub motors, tap BOOST to open the thruster valve, BRAKE to stop. Drag the view to orbit, pinch to zoom. The timeline replays a run.
