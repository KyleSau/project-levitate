Physics runs at 1,000 steps per second in real time: rigid-body dynamics, six skirted air-cushion compartments with variable-pitch lift fans, skirt leakage, rear thrust fans, aerodynamic and skirt drag, ground contact, water buoyancy and a 24S14P lithium-ion pack with voltage sag and current limits. LV-H1 air-cushion hoverboard

A 200 lb rider on a 1.8 m board that floats on a skirted air cushion and can hop about a metre. Everything you see is computed in real time.

  1. LIFT spins up the cushion fans.
  2. RIDER steps you on or off. Watch the ride height and power.
  3. Joystick: up/down = forward/reverse, left/right = turn.
  4. Hold HOP to jump; let go to come down on the cushion.
  5. LIFT again to land on the skids.
